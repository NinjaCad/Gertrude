How to Play:

  1. On their turn a player will play a card by pressing the "play card" button
  2. If the card played matches the card rank displayed slap the card using the "slap card" button as quickly as possible
  3. The player who slaps the card last adds the entire stack to their hand
  4. If a player slaps the card when the card played and the displayed rank do not match they take the entire stack
To Win:

  1. Once your hand is empty be the first to slap the deck when appropriate to win the game
